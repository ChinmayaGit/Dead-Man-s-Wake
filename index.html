CAPTAIN'S MANUAL & CONTROLS

⛵ Sailing & Speed
Use Sails Up / Down (or W/S / D-Pad) to shift between Furl (0), Half Sail, and Full Sail (Max). Watch the wind vane on your compass: sailing with the wind grants massive speed!
☸️ Steering Modes
Choose between the authentic Helm Wheel or tap ◀ ▶ Steer Buttons. Mobile defaults to Left/Right buttons for agile maneuvering, and PC defaults to the Wheel.
💥 Broadside Cannons
Fire Port (Left) or Starboard (Right) cannons with buttons, triggers (LT/RT), or Space/F keys. Red aim arcs show the firing cone. Lead your shots ahead of moving enemy ships!
⚡ Tactical Dodges
Forward Dodge: Quick speed surge to evade cannon volleys or cut off enemy ships.
Reverse Dodge: Emergency reverse brake to slip behind enemy broadsides.
⚓ Prow Ramming Strike (Capped at 30%)
Charge forward at high speed (>= 3.5 knots) bow-first into an enemy ship to deal up to 30% devastating ramming damage! Your reinforced prow absorbs the impact with 0 recoil damage. Ships must back away / disengage before another ram hit can be dealt!
🪙 Sunk Ships & Salvage Plunder
When you sink an enemy flagship, it breaks into floating treasure crates & rum barrels. Sail close to salvage them for Gold and Hull Repairs!
⚔️ 1v1 Multiplayer Naval Duels
Challenge friends peer-to-peer (P2P WebRTC)! Host or join with a 5-letter code. Last ship standing wins the high seas bounty.
